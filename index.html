SnapPairWear Privacy Policy

Effective: 22 October 2026

SnapPairWear helps you catalogue the clothes you own, combine them into outfits and remember what works. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

Your wardrobe stays on your iPhone. SnapPairWear has no account and no server, and it does not collect, sell or share your information. It uses no outside AI service, no advertising and no analytics. Garment and outfit suggestions use Apple Intelligence on your iPhone, and your wardrobe never leaves it.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your wardrobe: the garments, outfits, photos, ratings, comfort notes and other details you enter. They are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app.
  • Photos: the camera and your photo library are used only when you choose to add or change a photo. Only the photo you pick is saved; its location and other hidden details are removed.
  • Photo preparation: fixing the lighting and removing the background are done entirely on your iPhone by Apple's built-in technology. Photos are never uploaded for them.
  • Garment suggestions: when you add a garment, Apple Intelligence on your iPhone looks at its photo to suggest a category, colours and a short description for you to check. This happens entirely on your iPhone; the photo is not sent to Apple or anyone else, and nothing is saved unless you save the garment. You can turn Apple Intelligence off in Settings, which stops both garment and outfit suggestions.
  • Outfit suggestions: when you tap Suggest Outfit on New Outfit, Apple Intelligence on your iPhone completes the outfit from your own garments, using their descriptions, colours and ratings and the ratings of your saved outfits. This happens entirely on your iPhone; nothing is sent to Apple or anyone else, and nothing is saved unless you save the outfit, which keeps the suggestion's reason and tips in its notes. Suggestions are made only when you ask for one.
  • Your wardrobe is included in your iPhone's own backups, such as iCloud Backup, under Apple's terms.

What leaves your iPhone

The app sends nothing anywhere by itself. Information leaves your iPhone only when you choose:

  • Wardrobe backups: when you back up your wardrobe, you choose where the backup file goes. If you save it to iCloud Drive or another service, or send it to someone, that copy is handled by that service or person.

Testing with TestFlight

While SnapPairWear is in testing, Apple's TestFlight may collect crash reports and basic device information, and anything you choose to send as feedback, such as screenshots and comments. Apple shares this with the developer to fix problems. This is collected by Apple, not by SnapPairWear, and is covered by Apple's TestFlight terms and privacy policy.

Deleting your information

  • Delete single garments or outfits in the app, or use Delete All Outfits and Delete All Garments in Settings.
  • Deleting the app deletes your wardrobe from your iPhone.
  • Backup files you saved are not deleted with the app; delete them wherever you saved them.

Because nothing is stored on our side, there is nothing for us to delete or hand over.

Children

SnapPairWear is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If SnapPairWear ever changes how it handles information, for example by adding online features, this policy will be updated before that change, with a new effective date, and you will be asked for consent in the app before any personal information is sent to a third party.
